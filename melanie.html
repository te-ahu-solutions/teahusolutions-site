Founder, Te Ahu Solutions and AHU

Melanie Sweet

Commercial rigour. Kaupapa Māori depth.

I build ventures, and I help hapū, whānau and founders build theirs. Through Te Ahu Solutions I work alongside a few ventures at a time. Through AHU, I'm putting the same thinking into more people's hands.

Behind it is almost forty years across very different worlds: a commercial career in Australia's oil and gas, mining, engineering and construction sectors, then, since coming home in 2006, government, iwi and hapū. For the past ten years my commitment has been hapū development and a growing Māori economy.

How I work

Honest advice, shared accountability

The feedback I hear most is that I'm honest. I believe good work rests on transparency, accountability and people taking responsibility for their part, and I bring that to every engagement, even when it isn't the easy conversation.

Honesty first

A straight view of what's working and what isn't, said with respect.

Accountability

Clear roles, clear decisions, and everyone owning their part, leaders included.

Capability that stays

Growing your people's skills and capacity, so the gains last long after I've gone.

Smarter ways of working

Cutting through duplication and confusion so people can do their jobs well, with less stress.

Leadership should bring people together and put the kaupapa before ego. I work best with leaders who want honest advice and are ready to act on it.

What I bring to a venture

Depth where it counts

  • Commercial delivery. Procurement, contracts, risk and capital works, including managing more than 140 international suppliers on a major industrial plant refurbishment.
  • Property and investment. Long-term property and investment planning across a regional network of kura, shaped by kura and hapū aspirations.
  • Hapū and Māori enterprise. Hapū capability, Māori economic development, and helping establish a Māori farming collective.
  • Governance and partnerships. Board roles, executive leadership, and Crown–iwi relationships built on trust.

Founder

Melanie Sweet

Melanie founded Te Ahu in 2013. For Melanie, Te Ahu was about finding her pathway to her roots: the whakapapa that connects her to her tūpuna and whenua.

She moved to Australia at ten and spent twenty-five years there, where mentors who saw her potential early gave her a strong professional foundation. Coming home to Aotearoa in 2006 reconnected her with Te Ao Māori and with who she is.

Today she brings both together: professional rigour and a deep respect for tikanga, in service of people and organisations navigating change. She is also the founder of AHU, a guided coaching experience for individuals.

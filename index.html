Founder

Melanie Sweet

Melanie founded Te Ahu in 2013. For Melanie, Te Ahu was about finding her pathway to her roots: the whakapapa that connects her to her tūpuna and whenua.

She moved to Australia at ten and spent twenty-five years there, where mentors who saw her potential early gave her a strong professional foundation. Coming home to Aotearoa in 2006 reconnected her with Te Ao Māori and with who she is.

For the past ten years she has committed that experience to hapū development, bringing her commercial and corporate background to the forefront for the benefit of Māori and the growing Māori economy. Today she brings both worlds together: professional rigour and a deep respect for tikanga, helping hapū, whānau and founders build ventures that last. She is also the founder of AHU, a guided coaching experience for individuals.

"I work best with leaders who want honest advice and are ready to act on it."Melanie Sweet

Questions

Good questions to ask

Do you work with anyone who gets in touch?

No, and that's deliberate. We take on a small number of engagements at a time and choose them carefully. The first conversation is about whether we're the right fit for each other.

Can we talk before we commit?

Yes. The first conversation is free, up to 30 minutes, so we can both decide whether we're the right fit. After that, scoping, advice and proposals are paid work, under our Terms of Engagement.

Who will I work with?

Te Ahu Solutions is led by its founder, Melanie Sweet, who stays involved in every engagement. Depending on the venture, we bring in trusted people with the skills it needs: right people, right skills, right kaupapa.

Do you only work with Māori organisations?

No. Hapū and whānau ventures are at the heart of our work, and we also work with founders starting new businesses. Our practice is grounded in tikanga, so if that aligns with your venture and we're the right fit, then let's kōrero.

Where do you work?

We work remotely, from Te Tai Tokerau. If you'd like us there in person, that's your call, and we'll agree it up front.

How do you charge?

It depends on the work. After our first conversation we'll propose a clear scope and either a fixed fee or a day rate, agreed in writing before we start. No surprises.

How long does an engagement take?

Anything from a single wānanga to several months alongside you. We'll agree what's needed at the start, and we'll be honest if you need less than you think.

Do you respond to tenders?

Sometimes, when the kaupapa is a good fit. Most of our work comes from direct conversations and the people who know our work.

Is what we share kept confidential?

Yes. We treat everything you share as confidential, and we're happy to sign a confidentiality agreement.

How does AHU fit with Te Ahu Solutions?

AHU is a Te Ahu Solutions product. It brings the same approach to individuals: a guided coaching experience that helps people make sense of what matters and shape their next step. Visit ahucoach.com.
